The value model

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × seasoning × breadth

Category multiple

The median market cap ÷ TVL of the live protocols in the same category, ratios trimmed to 0.005–20 first. Multiples cluster by business model — liquid staking trades near 0.04×, bridges near 0.56× — so a flat rate would misprice almost everything. A category with fewer than 3 live protocols falls back to the global median.

Growth

1 + 0.45 × tanh(7-day TVL change ÷ 30). A rising protocol is worth a premium; tanh keeps one hot week from overwhelming the base. No recorded change means ×1.

Seasoning

0.75 rising to 1.20 over two years listed with no token recorded. Longevity is the brief's own signal: a protocol that has run for years tokenless has users a token can be distributed to. No listing date means a flat ×0.90 — unknown is not young.

Breadth

+4% per extra chain beyond the first, capped at five. More chains means more users on the day a token exists.

What this board is honest about

  • “No token recorded” describes this snapshot, not the world: a protocol may have a token the data does not show, and nothing here claims otherwise.
  • Implied values are estimates built only from the snapshot's own figures — TVL, 7-day change, listing date, chains. No prices, no social signals, no outside data.
  • Centralised exchanges and versioned deployments (a “V2” of a listed protocol) are not ranked: an exchange's value is its off-chain business, and a version is not a separate future token.
  • A farm plan earns a genuine usage history. No plan can promise an airdrop, and none here does.
